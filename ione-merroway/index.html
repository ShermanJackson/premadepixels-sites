About

A long way home.

Ione Merroway grew up on the low edge of Charleston, singing in rooms that were never quite meant for performance. Rooted In Him is her first collection: eight songs recorded in a borrowed living room, a single microphone, and late-afternoon light across the floor.

The record takes its name and its stillness from one image — something held, quietly, in the middle of an empty field of light. The songs that followed are about remaining, and about the patience it takes to do so.

The songs are about faith that stays through quiet seasons.
