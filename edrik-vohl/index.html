About

From the ruins up.

Edrik Vohl writes from a rented warehouse on the edge of Rotterdam. After the Fall is his first full-length: part score, part ruin-song, cut over fourteen nights from cracked piano, low strings, and a vocal that never quite asks for mercy.

The record takes its world from a single image — a face breaking open in the last light of a city. What followed was a sequence of songs about what remains when the towers go dark.
